Early warning, before the client calls

Each signal is watched against its own baseline. When one bends, the system names the likely cause, the play to run and who owns it, often while the overall score still looks fine. Growth signals surface the same way, because an account that is ready to expand deserves the same speed as one that is slipping.

From concern to meeting in one click

Seeing the problem is half the job. Readout turns a concern into the next steps: it summarizes what is happening, builds the agenda in our standard format, drafts the client deck and prepares the calendar invite. The account team reviews and sends; nothing goes out on its own.

Are outcomes following the work?

Account health is half the picture. The other half is whether patients get better. For each account, Readout lines up the care being delivered against the clinical results and says plainly whether one is following the other. Across the whole portfolio, the same data shows which parts of a program move outcomes. Blood pressure is one example; the same analysis runs on every monitoring device.

Switch accounts to compare a healthy program with one that is slipping.

What the client sees

Clients get their own view, refreshed every morning, instead of waiting for a quarterly review. The numbers are the same ones the team works from, written for the client: what is working, what needs a decision and what we are doing about it.
